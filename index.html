01 · The gap

This is a reading problem, not a drilling problem.

Two thirds of every barrel ever discovered in the United States is still down there. Not undiscovered — discovered, measured, written down, and walked past because the economics, the completion technology or the attention of the day never reached it.

01

Logged once, in 1958.

Vintage raster logs that were never digitized, never normalized across vendors, and never compared with what the well two sections over actually produced.

02

Cased off, not condemned.

Pay above and below the target gets cased off on the way to the zone somebody was paid to drill. The log still saw it. The report never mentioned it.

03

Evaluated by hand, one section at a time.

A geologist works through sections at human speed. There are hundreds of thousands of them, and the ones nobody got to are exactly where the value hides.

An interval the original operator cased off. The engine flags it because its signature matches intervals that produced in the same geology — illustrative, not a specific well.

02 · The engine

Four systems. One ranked list.

AIsopach is not a dashboard. It is an engine that ends in a ranked, title-cleared, economically screened target list — and it runs continuously, not per project.

E-01In production

Reads what the archive already knows

Raster and digital logs across vintages, vendors and units, pulled into one comparable signal. The engine flags intervals whose response matches what actually produced elsewhere — not what the original operator happened to be looking for.

E-02In production

Trained on outcomes, not on theory

Production history is the label. The engine learns which intervals produced, in which geology, under which completion — so the ranking sharpens every time a new well reports.

E-03Live today

The landman suite, already running

Runsheets, chains of title and net-mineral-acre positions assembled out of county records automatically. This is the part that is built and in use today: runsheet turnaround drops from weeks to [HOURS], and land spend falls [YOUR %] against contract-landman rates.

E-04In production

Ranked targets, with the math attached

Each target arrives with interval economics, resolved ownership and a recommended structure, then goes to Fifth Meridian Energy to acquire — royalty, minerals, non-op or operated.
